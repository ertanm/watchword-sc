Watchword — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Watchword translates existing Netflix and YouTube subtitles and lets you save words and phrases for local review. Watchword has no user accounts, analytics or backend of its own. Translation and optional licensing use the services below. We do not sell data or use it for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or lending.

Translation and website content

When enabled on a supported watch page, Watchword reads subtitle text for its overlay. Depending on the mode, subtitle lines are sent automatically for translation. Dictionary words/phrases and extension preview samples may also be translated. HTTPS requests send text and source/target language codes to Google's public translate endpoint at translate.googleapis.com. Text appears in the request URL. This is not an account-based Google Cloud Translation integration.

Watchword does not attach your name, email, library or license key to translation requests. Google receives normal network connection information, including IP address. Google's handling and retention follow its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Local library and settings

Settings, saved vocabulary and review progress are stored in chrome.storage.local, not synced to a Watchword server. Entries can include word/phrase, translation, dictionary example, subtitle context and its available translation, language pair, video title, URL, platform, observed playback time and review dates. Watchword does not collect a general history of visited websites. Saved video references are part of your local library. Text used to obtain translations is transmitted as described above.

  • settings: language pair, interface language, subtitle mode and study/display preferences.
  • saved_v1: vocabulary, context/source information and review progress.
  • cache_v2: subtitle/dictionary translations. Cached results can work offline; uncached requests need a connection.
  • operations_v1: up to 64 local operation receipts preventing duplicate saves/review answers on retry. These do not contain subtitle or vocabulary text and are not transmitted.
  • license_v2: entered license key, activation identifier, plan/status and validation time. Older local license records may be migrated here. Keys and activation identifiers are reused in licensing requests.

Optional licensing and payments

Free use without a stored key does not automatically contact Polar. Entering a key, including an invalid one, can contact api.polar.sh. Stored keys may be revalidated even after entitlement expires and the interface shows Free.

Activation, validation and deactivation send the key, organization identifier and, when applicable, activation identifier or device label "Watchword" to Polar over HTTPS. Keys are in request bodies, not URLs. Polar also receives normal connection information. Successful deactivation removes the local key; failed requests retain it for retry.

Checkout and the customer portal open Polar's pages. Polar handles customer/payment information there; Watchword does not receive or store card details. See https://polar.sh/legal/privacy. This describes the existing integration, not a guarantee that paid plans or production checkout have completed release acceptance.

Feedback and external links

Feedback opens a draft in your email application. Watchword does not automatically send it or attach your library, subtitle history or key. If you send it, support receives your email and included information to respond and investigate. Do not include keys or sensitive information. You can contact support to request deletion of correspondence.

Anki, Buy Me a Coffee, checkout and portal links open external sites only when selected; those sites apply their own privacy practices. Watchword does not load their tracking scripts into the extension. Optional pronunciation uses browser speech synthesis; processing depends on the browser, operating system and selected voice.

Permissions

  • storage: local settings, vocabulary, review, cache, operation receipts and licensing state.
  • https://www.netflix.com/* and https://www.youtube.com/*: read subtitles and show the overlay on supported watch pages. Navigation pages match because these sites can open videos without reloading the document.
  • https://translate.googleapis.com/*: translation and dictionary requests.
  • https://api.polar.sh/*: optional license activation, validation and deactivation.

Watchword does not record clicks, keystrokes or mouse movements as analytics. Scripts are packaged with the extension; translation responses are data, not remotely downloaded executable code.

Retention and removal

Local settings/library remain until changed, cleared with available controls or removed with the extension. The translation cache is bounded and older entries can be evicted. Uninstall deletes local extension storage, not previously exported files, provider records or support emails. Export/backup availability depends on the existing plan; Free users should not assume uninstall is reversible. Disabling the extension preserves local data.
