Watchword — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Watchword is a browser extension that translates the subtitles on Netflix and YouTube — showing them in your language, in both languages, or with a dictionary card on any word. This policy explains exactly what data Watchword handles and what it does not.

The short version: Watchword has no accounts, no analytics, and no servers of its own. It does not track you, profile you, or sell or share your data. The only data it sends anywhere is the subtitle text and individual words you choose to translate, which go to Google's public translate service so they can be translated and shown back to you.

What Watchword accesses and why

  • Subtitle text and hovered words (website content). On a Netflix or YouTube watch page, Watchword reads the on-screen subtitle text to display it, and sends subtitle lines (and any word you hover) to Google's public translate endpoint (translate.googleapis.com) to be translated and shown back to you. It is used solely to provide the translation feature you requested.
  • Your settings. Your language pair, subtitle mode, and study options are saved locally with chrome.storage.
  • Words you save. A saved word is stored with its translation, the subtitle line it appeared in, a link back to the video, and your review schedule. All of it stays on your device — there is no account it could be uploaded to.
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro or Lifetime. The key you paste in is sent to Polar (api.polar.sh), our payment provider, to check that it is valid, then stored locally. Free users never contact Polar at all.
  • A local translation cache. Translations are cached on your device so repeats are instant and work offline. This cache never leaves your device.

What Watchword does NOT do

  • No personally identifiable information, authentication, financial, health, location, or browsing-history data.
  • No recording of clicks, keystrokes, or mouse movement as data.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or transferring of data for advertising or any purpose unrelated to translation.
  • No use of data for creditworthiness or lending.

Third-party services

Watchword talks to exactly two outside services, and to nothing else.

  • Google Translate — subtitle text and hovered words are sent there to be translated. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's privacy policy. Watchword sends only the text to be translated and does not attach your identity to it.
  • Polar — only if you buy Pro or Lifetime, and only to validate your license key. Polar is the Merchant of Record and handles the payment on its own pages; Watchword never sees or stores your payment details. Polar's privacy policy.

The free popup also shows one of two plain outbound links (Anki and Buy Me a Coffee). They are ordinary links: nothing is loaded from those sites, no tracker or redirect sits behind them, and nothing happens unless you click. They are named here because a policy that says "two services" while the interface shows two more addresses is a policy that invites the wrong conclusion.

Permissions

  • storage — save settings, saved words, and the translation cache locally.
  • Access to netflix.com and youtube.com — read subtitle text and display the overlay. The match is broader than the watch pages because both sites are single-page apps: navigating to a video does not reload the page, so the extension has to already be present to notice. It does nothing on any other page.
  • Access to translate.googleapis.com — fetch translations.
  • Access to api.polar.sh — validate a license key, for paying users only.

Data retention and removal

Your settings, saved words, review schedule and translation cache live only on your device. Removing the extension deletes all of Watchword's locally stored data. If you want to keep your saved words first, the Saved Words page can export them to a file.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
